## Priority Framework

| Priority | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **P1** | Widely adopted enterprise tool, major competitors have certified connector, significant ACE deal/churn risk without it |
| **P2** | Strong enterprise use case, growing demand, Workato/Boomi coverage, moderate competitive gap |
| **P3** | Niche/specialised, lower competitive pressure, good-to-have |

## Connector Status Legend

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `Managed` | IBM-supported, SLA-backed, production-grade |
| `Community → Managed` | Being promoted from community tier to managed |
| `Net-New` | Brand new connector being built from scratch |
| `Community` | Community-maintained, no IBM Support path, no SLA |
| `India Stack` | Part of India regulatory/payments infrastructure |
