Enterprises routinely use Zapier, Make, or custom scripting to connect Zoom with Salesforce, ServiceNow, and Jira because their integration platforms lack a native connector. Dedicated integration services vendors (Achieva, FlowMondo) have emerged specifically to build Salesforce-Zoom integrations — direct evidence of unmet demand for an out-of-the-box iPaaS connector. IBM App Connect customers in these enterprises have no native option and are at churn risk to Workato and Boomi for Zoom-dependent workflows.
